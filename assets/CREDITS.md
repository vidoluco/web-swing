# Asset credits

Every third-party asset that ships in `public/` for the city look. All are CC0 (public domain), so attribution is
not required, but it is given here. Textures were resized to 1024 px and re-encoded (colour and roughness packed
into one JPEG, normal map as two channels); the models are used as downloaded.
